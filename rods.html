The rods it knows.

Standard rods just work. These have reels of their own.

Noiseform Pinion's Aria Verdant Oath Ruinous Oath Luminescent Oath Poseidon's Lance Bellona's Waraxe Apollo's Sunshot Cinder Block Rod Requiem Splitbranch Twig

Noiseform

A glowing emblem sits behind the bar, and coloured warnings name a zone to move to.

FISCHXR reads the warning and takes the bar there.

Pinion's Aria

Notes fall onto the reel while you reel.

It catches the notes without losing the fish.

Verdant Oath

Wooden blocks, a shrinking green zone, and a penalty for touching the wood.

It keeps the fish in the green, even as the zone shrinks.

Ruinous Oath

The bar shrinks and turns from white to deep red.

It follows every shade.

Luminescent Oath

The bar shrinks and turns from white to deep blue.

It follows every shade.

Poseidon's Lance

A blue sweet spot sits in the middle of the bar.

It reads the whole bar and keeps the fish on the blue.

Bellona's Waraxe

Two reels at once, one on each mouse button.

It steers both at the same time.

Apollo's Sunshot

The bar turns almost black when the fish slips out.

It keeps reading the bar through the dark.

Cinder Block Rod

The bar fills the whole reel and never moves.

It just lets the reel run.

Requiemimproving

A reel style of its own.

Supported, and still being improved.

Splitbranch Twig

A reel that doesn't behave like the standard one.

Handled, tested and working.
